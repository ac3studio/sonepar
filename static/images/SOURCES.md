# Image sources

The chapter media in `chapter-01/` through `chapter-06/` came from the client-supplied `Sustainability_Flip_Cards` handoff. The `ch*-card*.png` Photoshop composites are visual references only: the kiosk uses their underlying photos and logos, and renders country labels as UI text. Some source cards are intentionally logo-only.

The six topic-menu photos in `topics/` came from the client-supplied `Smash` folder, in chapter order.

Chapter content follows `Sustainability-App _ Synoptique.docx` and subsequent client corrections. Chapter 4 card 1 uses the corrected electric-vehicle copy; card 2 still uses the earlier synoptique copy pending a replacement.
